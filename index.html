Portfolio episodes

A personal tool, not a public service.

Portfolio episodes turns my own daily business-news briefing into an audio episode and uploads it to my own YouTube channel, as private videos in a private playlist.

It runs only on my own computer and is used only by me. It asks Google for access to my YouTube account so it can upload videos, set their thumbnails and add them to a playlist on my channel. It does not access anyone else's account or data.
