Privacy policy: Portfolio episodes

Last updated: October 29, 2026

What this app is

Portfolio episodes is a personal tool used by a single person, its developer, to upload audio episodes of his own daily business briefing to his own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Google data it uses

With the account owner's permission (the YouTube scope), the app:

  • uploads videos to the signed-in user's own channel and sets their titles, descriptions, privacy status and thumbnails;
  • creates and updates a playlist on that channel and adds the uploaded videos to it;
  • reads the channel name and the user's own playlists, to find the right playlist.

It does not read, collect or change anything else in the account, and it does not access other users' data.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token issued by Google is stored only on the developer's own computer. No Google user data is sent to any server other than Google's, and none is sold, shared or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the computer also ends the app's access.
